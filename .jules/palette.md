## 2024-05-17 - Dynamic ARIA Live Regions for Toast Notifications
**Learning:** Toast notifications represent different severities that require different screen reader announcements. Using a static `aria-live` or `role` for all toasts can result in either overly aggressive interruptions (for simple success messages) or missed critical alerts (for errors). Decorative icons in toasts also cause confusing announcements ("check mark", "cross mark") before the actual message.
**Action:** When implementing toast or notification components, dynamically use `role="alert"` and `aria-live="assertive"` for error/warning types, and `role="status"` with `aria-live="polite"` for general/success updates to ensure proper screen reader announcements. Additionally, always apply `aria-hidden="true"` to decorative symbols or emojis within the toast and provide explicit `aria-label` attributes for icon-only close buttons.
